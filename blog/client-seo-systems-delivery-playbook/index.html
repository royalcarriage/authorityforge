Client SEO Systems Delivery Playbook

How to sell and ship hub/spoke authority systems for client brands.

Direct answer: Selling and shipping hub/spoke authority systems for client brands involves a structured process that moves from deep discovery and architectural planning to phased content creation, a clear handoff runbook, and tiered pricing models, all designed to build foundational SEO authority and drive organic growth.

Discovery questions

Effective client SEO begins with thorough discovery. Understand their business objectives, target audience, and competitive landscape. Ask about their ideal customer's pain points and the core problems their products or services solve. Inquire about their existing content assets and any internal resources for writing or development. Crucially, clarify their budget and timeline expectations early to scope the project appropriately.

Architecture deliverable

Following discovery, the architecture deliverable is your client's SEO blueprint. This includes detailed keyword research identifying core hub topics and supporting spoke topics. A content audit maps existing assets to potential new structures. You'll define topic clusters, outlining the main hub pages and their associated spoke pages. Provide clear URL structure recommendations and a comprehensive internal linking strategy. Content brief templates for each page type ensure consistency, and a visual sitemap illustrates the hierarchy for easy understanding.

Page build order

Prioritize content creation for maximum impact. Begin with the core hub pages; these are the foundational pillars of authority and need to be comprehensive. Next, focus on high-priority spoke pages that address critical user intent or have strong commercial value. Gradually fill out the clusters with supporting spoke pages. Implement an iterative publishing schedule, releasing content in batches rather than waiting for every piece to be perfect. Ensure internal linking is established immediately as pages go live, and conduct technical SEO checks for indexability and performance.

Handoff runbook

A clear handoff ensures the client can maintain and scale the system. Provide a content calendar or roadmap for future content creation and a set of reusable content brief templates. Include detailed internal linking guidelines to preserve and grow the link structure. Set up and train them on a performance monitoring dashboard for tracking organic traffic, rankings, and conversions. Deliver comprehensive documentation outlining the architecture, strategy, and ongoing maintenance. Finally, provide access credentials to relevant tools like Google Search Console and their CMS.
