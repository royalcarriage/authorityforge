Weekly Cluster Audit Checklist for Content Sites

A repeatable weekly audit so hubs and spokes stay intentional and monetizable.

A weekly cluster audit involves exporting Google Search Console index coverage, mapping hub ownership to every monetizable URL, identifying and addressing thin content by merging or deleting, strategically adding new commercial spokes, and consistently measuring impressions for the entire cluster. This routine keeps your topical clusters focused, current, and aligned with revenue goals.

Export index coverage

Start your weekly audit by pulling fresh index coverage data from Google Search Console (GSC). This provides a snapshot of how Google views your site's pages, highlighting any new issues that could affect cluster performance. Focus on the 'Pages' report under the 'Indexing' section.

Steps:

1. Navigate to Google Search Console for your property. 2. Select 'Indexing' > 'Pages' from the left-hand menu. 3. Set the date range filter to 'Last 7 days' to identify recent changes or new issues. 4. Examine categories like 'Excluded by ‘noindex’ tag', 'Soft 404', 'Duplicate, submitted URL not selected as canonical', and 'Page with redirect'. These often point to content that Google is struggling to understand or index correctly, directly impacting your cluster's authority. 5. Export the data to a CSV or Google Sheets for easier sorting and analysis. Pay close attention to URLs within your target cluster path (e.g., /systems/topical-clusters/).

By isolating issues to the past week, you can address indexing problems before they compound and affect your cluster's visibility. A 'soft 404' on a key spoke, for instance, can quickly degrade the perceived authority of its parent hub.

Map hub ownership

Every monetizable spoke in your content cluster needs a clear, assigned hub. This ensures logical site structure, proper internal linking, and concentrated topic authority. Without explicit hub ownership, spokes can become orphaned, diluting their potential and confusing search engines.

Steps:

1. Open your preferred spreadsheet (e.g., Google Sheets) or a dedicated content inventory tool. 2. List all URLs exported from GSC, especially those within your cluster path. 3. For each URL, identify its primary hub. For example, a spoke like /systems/topical-clusters/ai-content-audit/ should clearly map to the /systems/topical-clusters/ hub. 4. Create columns for 'URL', 'Assigned Hub URL', and 'Status' (e.g., 'Live', 'Needs Review', 'Merge Candidate'). 5. Review any URLs that appear to be spokes but lack a clear hub assignment or are linked to an irrelevant hub.

Consider your method for mapping:

| Criteria | Manual Spreadsheet (e.g., Google Sheets) | API Integration (e.g., Screaming Frog + GSC API) | | :-------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | | Setup Time | Low (start immediately) | Moderate (API keys, configuration) | | Cost | Free (with existing tools) | Varies (tool subscription, developer time) | | Scalability | Limited (best for smaller sites or specific clusters) | High (automates data pull and initial mapping) | | Accuracy | High (human review ensures intent) | High (rule-based, but may require manual override for edge cases) | | Control | Complete (you define every rule and assignment) | Good (can set up custom rules, but relies on tool's capabilities) | | Best For | Deep dives into specific clusters, smaller sites, first-time audits | Large content sites, recurring audits, integrating multiple data sources |

Regardless of your tool, the goal is a definitive list where every spoke knows its home. This clarity is foundational for effective internal linking and content strategy.

Kill or merge thin URLs

Thin content saps authority, wastes crawl budget, and offers little to no value to users. Identify these underperforming pages within your cluster and take decisive action. A weekly audit prevents accumulation of such content.

Definition of "Thin": A page is often considered thin if it meets one or more of these criteria: * Less than 200 words of unique, informative content. * No clear purpose or target keyword. * Zero or very few internal links to or from other cluster pages. * Consistently low impressions and clicks in GSC over several months. * High bounce rate and low average time on page in analytics.

Decision Criteria:

| Action | Description | When to Use | | :--------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | | Keep | Update and improve the page, add more detail, visuals, and internal links. | The page has potential, targets a valuable keyword, or serves a specific (even if niche) user need, but is currently underperforming due to lack of depth or optimization. | | Merge | Consolidate the content into a more authoritative, existing hub or spoke. Implement a 301 redirect. | The page's topic is covered better by another page, or its content is too brief to stand alone. Combining it adds value to the destination page. | | Kill | Delete the page entirely and return a 404 (Not Found) or 410 (Gone) status code.
