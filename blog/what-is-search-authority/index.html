What Is Search Authority? A Practical Definition

Search authority is earned topical ownership plus trust signals that let your pages win more queries over time.

Direct answer: Search authority is the degree to which search engines treat your site as a reliable owner of a topic—built from content depth, link/entity signals, technical access, and consistent user satisfaction.

Authority is not domain rating alone

Metrics help, but authority shows up as: more queries covered, higher average positions, and better CTR at the same position because your SERP promise matches the page.
