Free vs Pro vs Agency: Who Should Pay for AuthorityForge

Honest SKU guide so operators self-select Free, Pro, or Agency OS — and sales can close without hype.

AuthorityForge offers Free, Pro, and Agency tiers to match different operational needs. The Free tier suits solo explorers for initial testing. Pro powers individual operators and small teams for faster output and deeper analysis. Agency provides white-label capabilities, team management, and client reporting for client-facing SEO firms.

Jobs to be done

Operators use AuthorityForge to streamline content creation, keyword research, and SERP analysis. The core job is to produce high-quality, SEO-optimized content with AI assistance, reducing manual effort and speeding up publishing cycles. Different roles require different scales of operation.

For example, a solo blogger might need to: * Generate 5 blog post ideas around a specific topic. * Draft an outline for one article using AI. * Check the top 10 SERP results for a target keyword.

A small in-house SEO team or agency might need to: * Map content clusters for 20+ topics per month. * Generate full article drafts for 10-15 pieces weekly. * Track keyword performance across multiple projects and clients.

Your operational scale and specific workflow determine which AuthorityForge tier aligns best with your "jobs to be done."

Free limits

The Free tier is designed for initial exploration and basic task execution. It allows new users to test core functionalities and understand the platform's workflow without commitment. This tier provides a restricted set of resources, suitable for single-user, low-volume tasks.

Free tier constraints include: * AI Credits: 50 AI credits per month. * Active Projects: 1 active project at a time. * SERP Analyzer: Limited to 3 keyword analyses per day. * Content Editor: Basic text generation and optimization suggestions.

This tier is ideal for an operator who needs to validate an idea or complete a single, small content task. It's a starting point, not a long-term production environment.

| Feature / Limit | Free Tier | Pro Tier | | :------------------ | :-------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------- | | AI Credits | 50 / month | 500 / month | | Active Projects | 1 | Unlimited | | SERP Analyzer | 3 keywords / day | Unlimited, with advanced filters | | Content Editor | Basic AI assistance | Advanced AI, outline builder, content scoring | | Team Members | 1 (solo operator) | 1 (solo operator) |

Pro velocity

The Pro tier is built for operators who need to increase output and efficiency. This tier removes many of the Free tier's limitations, providing the capacity for consistent content production and deeper analytical work. It's suitable for individual SEOs, content managers, or small teams managing a single brand's content strategy.

Pro tier benefits include: * Increased AI Credits: 500 AI credits per month, allowing for more extensive content generation. * Unlimited Projects: Manage as many content initiatives or client projects as needed. * Advanced SERP Analyzer: Access to competitor content outlines, keyword gaps, and historical data. * Content Cluster Builder: A dedicated module for mapping out topical authority. * Priority Support: Faster response times for operational queries.

An operator on the Pro tier can manage an entire content calendar, from ideation to optimization, for a medium-sized website or several smaller ones. For example, generating a full 2000-word article draft typically consumes about 20-30 AI credits, making 500 credits sufficient for significant monthly output.

| Feature / Limit | Pro Tier | Agency Tier | | :-------------------- | :--------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- | | AI Credits | 500 / month | 5,000 / month | | Active Projects | Unlimited | Unlimited | | SERP Analyzer | Unlimited, advanced filters | Unlimited, advanced filters, client reports | | Content Cluster Builder | Included | Included, with team collaboration | | White-Label Reporting | No | Yes, custom branding | | Team Members | 1 (solo operator) | Up to 10 | | API Access | No | Yes |

Agency white-label

The Agency tier caters to SEO agencies, consultancies, and larger marketing teams managing multiple clients. This tier extends the Pro features with capabilities essential for client management, team collaboration, and brand consistency. It transforms AuthorityForge into a client-facing asset.

Key Agency tier features: * High AI Credit Pool: 5,000 AI credits per month, accommodating high-volume client work. * Team Member Management: Add up to 10 team members, assign roles, and track individual contributions. * White-Label Client Dashboards: Present AuthorityForge data and reports under your agency's brand, using a custom domain. * API Access: Integrate AuthorityForge functionalities directly into your existing tools or client portals. *Centralized Billing
