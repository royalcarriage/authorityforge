Topical Maps for SaaS and AI Tools

Build a topical map that separates feature docs, comparisons, and educational hubs.

Direct answer: Map three layers—product (features), market (comparisons/alternatives), and education (how-to)—then assign a single URL owner in each cell.

Common failure

Blog posts that clone the homepage keyword. Fix with redirects or retitling to question intent.

Tools we research (affiliate when live): Semrush · Ahrefs · Surfer · all programs
