AI Writing Assistants for SEO Operators

How to pick AI writing tools that speed drafts without creating thin, unpublishable pages.

Direct answer: Use AI writers for outlines and first drafts, then enforce human or frontier-model edit gates, one intent per URL, and measurement before scale.

What “good” looks like

  • Strong brief → draft → edit workflow
  • Controls for tone, sources, and forbidden claims
  • Exports that keep headings and internal link placeholders

Evaluation criteria

  1. Can it follow a cluster brief?
  2. Does it invent stats?
  3. Can you template CTAs and disclosures?
