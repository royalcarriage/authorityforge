Affiliate disclosure

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Direct answer: Some links on AuthorityForge are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Recommendations are based on usefulness for AI productivity and practical SEO systems—not commission size alone.

What this means

  • We may partner with software, hosting, analytics, SEO, or AI tool vendors.
  • Affiliate cookies or tracking parameters may be used by those partners.
  • You never pay more because you used our link (standard affiliate economics).

How we choose products

  1. Relevance to search authority, content systems, or AI productivity workflows
  2. Whether we would use the tool (or have evaluated its category carefully)
  3. Clear pricing and terms for operators

If a product is a poor fit, we will not recommend it just for a higher payout.

How affiliate links are marked

  • Sitewide notice in the disclosure bar (every page)
  • This dedicated disclosure page linked from the footer
  • Inline notes on reviews/comparisons when a specific partner link is used (e.g. “affiliate link”)

Display ads (future)

We may enable Google AdSense or similar after traffic quality is proven. Ads will not replace this affiliate disclosure. See monetization structure.
