Privacy policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Direct answer: AuthorityForge may collect standard web logs and analytics to improve content systems. We use cookies or similar tech for affiliates and, when enabled, advertising partners such as Google AdSense. We do not sell personal information.

Who we are

AuthorityForge is a public demonstration site for search-authority systems. Repository: github.com/royalcarriage/authorityforge.

Information we collect

  • Server/CDN logs (IP, user agent, referrer) for security and debugging
  • Analytics events (pages viewed, CTAs) if analytics is configured
  • Affiliate click parameters when you use partner links
  • Ad-related cookies/identifiers if display ads are enabled after AdSense approval

How we use information

  • Operate and improve the site and content systems
  • Measure organic performance and UX
  • Attribute affiliate referrals
  • Serve and measure ads (only when ads are turned on)

Google AdSense & advertising

When AdSense is enabled, Google and partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads (including personalized ads where allowed). Users can manage ad personalization via Google’s ad settings: adssettings.google.com.

See monetization structure.

EEA, UK, and Switzerland — consent (Google CMP)

Direct answer: For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use Google’s certified Consent Management Platform (AdSense Privacy & messaging) to collect choices about advertising partners and purposes before personalized ads are served.

  • Consent — allow ad partners / purposes as described in the message
  • Do not consent — decline in one step (limited / non-personalized ads path where applicable)
  • Manage options — granular control over purposes and vendors

The consent message is configured in Google AdSense and loads with the AdSense code on this site and other sites we attach to the same message. You can change or withdraw choices later via the message’s manage options (when shown) and Google ad settings.

Cookies

Essential cookies may be used by the host (Vercel/GitHub Pages). Affiliate networks and ad partners may set their own cookies. In EEA/UK/CH, non-essential advertising storage is gated by the consent choice above where Google CMP applies.

Third parties

  • Hosting: Vercel and/or GitHub Pages
  • Analytics: only if configured
  • Affiliates: partner networks when links are used
  • Advertising: Google AdSense when enabled

Data retention

Logs are retained as long as needed for security and operations, subject to host defaults.

Your choices

  • Browser controls for cookies

  • Contact via GitHub for privacy questions
