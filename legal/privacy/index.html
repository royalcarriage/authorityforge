Privacy policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Direct answer: AuthorityForge may collect standard web logs and analytics to improve content systems. We use cookies or similar tech for affiliates and, when enabled, advertising partners such as Google AdSense. We do not sell personal information.

Who we are

AuthorityForge is a public demonstration site for search-authority systems. Repository: github.com/royalcarriage/authorityforge.

Information we collect

  • Server/CDN logs (IP, user agent, referrer) for security and debugging
  • Analytics events (pages viewed, CTAs) if analytics is configured
  • Affiliate click parameters when you use partner links
  • Ad-related cookies/identifiers if display ads are enabled after AdSense approval

How we use information

  • Operate and improve the site and content systems
  • Measure organic performance and UX
  • Attribute affiliate referrals
  • Serve and measure ads (only when ads are turned on)

Google AdSense & advertising

When AdSense is enabled, Google and partners may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other sites. Users can manage ad personalization via Google’s ad settings: adssettings.google.com.

We will not enable live ads until ADS_ENABLED is set after account approval. See monetization structure.

Cookies

Essential cookies may be used by the host (Vercel/GitHub Pages). Affiliate networks and ad partners may set their own cookies.

Third parties

  • Hosting: Vercel and/or GitHub Pages
  • Analytics: only if configured
  • Affiliates: partner networks when links are used
  • Advertising: Google AdSense when enabled

Data retention

Logs are retained as long as needed for security and operations, subject to host defaults.

Your choices

  • Browser controls for cookies
  • Google ad settings for personalized ads
  • Contact via GitHub for privacy questions

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change.
