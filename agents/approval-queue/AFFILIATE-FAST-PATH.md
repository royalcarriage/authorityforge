## Skip — dead ends (do NOT waste time)
- **Ahrefs** — no public program since 2019 (invite-only). Editorial links only.
- **Screaming Frog** — no affiliate program exists.
- **Notion** — program CLOSED to new applicants (~Apr 2026). Revisit if reopened.
- **Amazon Associates** — instant entry but auto-dropped without 3 sales in 180d, and the SaaS tools aren't sold there. Secondary at best.

## Structural facts that change strategy
- **AdSense will NOT approve a *.vercel.app subdomain.** The `ca-pub-1959018852581373` "getting_ready" status cannot complete on the current host — a custom domain (~$12/yr) is REQUIRED for the AdSense stream, and materially raises affiliate approval odds too. This makes the domain purchase the highest-leverage $12 in the whole project.
- Network accounts (Impact/PartnerStack/FirstPromoter) approve instantly; it's the BRANDS that review manually. One Impact + one PartnerStack account covers most of the next wave.
