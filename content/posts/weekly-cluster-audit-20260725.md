**Direct answer:** A weekly cluster audit helps content sites ensure their hubs and spokes remain focused and monetizable. This process involves exporting index coverage, mapping ownership of hub pages, identifying and addressing thin content, adding commercial value through new spoke articles, and measuring the performance improvements.

## Export Index Coverage

Begin your audit by exporting the current index coverage for all clusters from Google Search Console (GSC). Focus on keywords with significant search volume that align with existing or planned topics. Use this data to understand which areas of your content are performing well and where there might be gaps in visibility.

## Map Hub Ownership

Assign ownership responsibility for each hub page among your team members. Ensure every hub has a designated owner who can manage its spoke articles, update the cluster map, and maintain overall strategy alignment with business goals. This ensures accountability and proactive management of content quality.

## Kill or Merge Thin URLs

Identify any thin content that does not contribute meaningfully to user engagement or search rankings. Remove non-essential pages or merge them into more substantial, relevant content within your clusters. Prioritize actions based on the potential impact on SEO metrics like click-through rates and time on page.

## Add One Commercial Spoke

Each week, add at least one new spoke article that has commercial intent and supports a hub page's theme. This could be through product reviews, affiliate links, or sponsored content. Ensure that the addition of commercial elements does not compromise user experience or violate Google's guidelines regarding paid partnerships.

## Measure Impressions

Regularly measure the impressions gained from your clusters to assess their growth potential and performance over time. Use tools like GSC and analytics platforms to track how changes in hub and spoke articles affect overall traffic and engagement metrics. Adjust strategies based on these insights for continuous improvement.
