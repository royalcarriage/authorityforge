**Direct answer:** Affiliates must disclose their relationships transparently to maintain reader trust and comply with search engine guidelines, which indirectly supports SEO efforts.

## Why disclosure is ranking-adjacent

Search engines like Google prioritize sites that provide clear, trustworthy content. While direct penalties for non-compliance may be rare, failing to disclose affiliate links can lead to a loss of credibility among users and search engines alike. This diminished trust can result in lower engagement metrics such as click-through rates (CTR) and dwell time, which are factors in ranking algorithms.

## Placement patterns

Effective placement involves integrating disclosures naturally into content without disrupting the user experience. Common locations include:

- At the start or end of reviews and product recommendations
- Within or next to affiliate link calls-to-action
- In a dedicated section like "Disclosure" or "Affiliate Note"

Placing the disclosure prominently but contextually relevant ensures that users see it before they engage with links, reducing risks associated with hidden disclosures.

## Wording

Clear wording is crucial for FTC compliance and consumer trust. Common phrases include:

- "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases"
- "This product review contains affiliate links to help support our work."

Using straightforward language that explains the nature of your relationship with the vendor can enhance transparency without being overly verbose.

## Common mistakes

Avoid these pitfalls to ensure compliance and maintain reader trust:
1. **Hidden disclosures:** Placing them at the bottom or in small print.
2. **Inconsistent disclosure:** Not disclosing every affiliate link consistently throughout content.
3. **Lack of clarity:** Using vague language that doesn't convey the affiliate relationship clearly.

Correcting these mistakes can prevent legal issues and improve user trust, leading to higher engagement rates and better rankings.
