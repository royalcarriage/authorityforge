To build a trustworthy affiliate stack, promote only tools you use daily, understand deeply, and can genuinely recommend. Clearly disclose all affiliate relationships at the top of your content to maintain audience trust and comply with regulations. Focus on tools that solve specific, recurring problems for SEO operators, ensuring they align with your content's focus.

## Criteria before links

Before you add an affiliate link to your content, apply a strict filter. Your reputation with hands-on SEO operators depends on recommending genuinely useful tools. Start by asking if you would pay for this tool yourself if the affiliate program didn't exist.

Here’s a checklist to evaluate potential affiliate partners:

*   **Personal Use:** Do you use this tool regularly in your own SEO workflows? Can you demonstrate its features and benefits from direct experience? If not, run a trial. For example, before promoting Surfer SEO, put it through a full content optimization cycle for at least two articles.
*   **Audience Fit:** Does the tool solve a real problem your audience faces? Is it relevant to the topics you typically cover on AuthorityForge? Promoting a local SEO tool on a site focused on enterprise SEO might not convert well.
*   **Commission Structure:** Understand the payout model. Is it a one-time payment or a recurring percentage? Recurring commissions from subscriptions are generally more valuable over time. Also, check the cookie duration; longer durations (e.g., 60-90 days) are better than short ones (e.g., 7 days).
*   **Support & Reliability:** Does the company offer good customer support? Are there frequent outages or bugs? A tool that constantly breaks will reflect poorly on your recommendation.
*   **Terms of Service:** Read the affiliate program's terms carefully. Some programs prohibit bidding on branded keywords, using specific promotional methods, or have high payout thresholds.

## Disclosure placement

Transparency is non-negotiable. Your audience needs to know when you might earn a commission. This builds trust and complies with regulations like the FTC's Endorsement Guides in the US.

Place your disclosure clearly and prominently:

1.  **Above the Fold:** The disclosure must be visible to the user without scrolling. The best practice is at the very top of the article, before any content begins.
2.  **Near the First Link:** Repeat a concise disclosure close to the first actual affiliate link within the content. This reinforces the message as they encounter a monetized element.
3.  **Clear Language:** Use plain, unambiguous language. Avoid jargon. Phrases like "This post contains affiliate links. If you make a purchase through these links, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you" are effective.
4.  **Dedicated Disclosure Page:** Link to a full affiliate disclosure page on your site (e.g., `/legal/affiliate-disclosure/`) for users who want more detail.
