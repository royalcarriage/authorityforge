The weekly cluster audit checklist for content sites ensures topical clusters remain intentional and monetizable by systematically reviewing index coverage, mapping hub ownership, removing thin content, adding commercial spokes, and measuring performance. This repeatable process prevents content decay and aligns cluster strategy with business goals, making content efforts more effective.

## Export index coverage

Start by extracting a complete list of your site's indexed URLs. This foundational data shows what Google currently recognizes, allowing you to identify pages that should or should not be part of your topical clusters
