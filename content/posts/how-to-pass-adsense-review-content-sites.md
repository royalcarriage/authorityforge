**Direct answer:** To pass AdSense review in 2026, content sites need to ensure compliance with Google's policies on privacy, navigation, and ad placement while also adhering to technical requirements such as having an ads.txt file.

## What Reviewers Look for

AdSense reviewers focus on several key aspects when evaluating a site. These include the presence of clear and compliant policy pages (Privacy Policy, Terms of Use), effective navigation that enhances user experience, value-dense content with minimal ad clutter, and adherence to Google's invalid traffic policies. The overall quality and relevance of your content are also crucial.

## Page Count Myth

Contrary to some misconceptions, the number of pages a site has is not a decisive factor in AdSense approval. What matters more is the depth and breadth of useful content provided along with proper navigation that makes it easy for users to find what they're looking for.

## Policy Pages

Ensuring your Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are clear, concise, and compliant is essential. These pages should be easily accessible from every page on your site through a footer or header menu item labeled appropriately (e.g., "Privacy," "Terms"). Compliance with legal standards relevant to data privacy laws in the countries where you operate can prevent rejection.

## ads.txt

The `ads.txt` file is crucial for transparency and preventing ad fraud. This text file, placed in your website’s root directory or `/static/`, lists authorized sellers of your inventory along with their contact details. Make sure it's correctly formatted as per Google's guidelines to avoid technical disqualification during the review process.

## After Approval

Once approved, maintaining compliance is key to sustaining AdSense revenue. Regularly monitor your site for issues related to invalid traffic and adjust your content strategy and monetization tactics based on performance data from Google Analytics and Search Console.
