To build an affiliate stack that converts and maintains trust, prioritize tools you genuinely use and recommend. Focus on solving specific workflow problems for your audience, ensuring the tool's value is clear and its pricing fair. Disclose affiliate relationships clearly and consistently, integrating them naturally into content that educates and assists users.

## Criteria before links

Before adding any affiliate link to your content, thoroughly vet the product. Your reputation is tied to every recommendation. A bad tool reflects poorly on you and erodes reader trust.

Start with your own workflow. Do you currently use this tool? Does it solve a specific problem you've encountered as an SEO operator? If you wouldn't pay for it yourself, or if you don't use it daily, it's likely not a good fit for your recommendations.

Consider the following checklist for each potential affiliate:

*   **Problem Solved:** Does it address a clear pain point for hands-on SEO operators? (e.g., "automates backlink monitoring," "simplifies content brief creation").
*   **Personal Experience:** Have you used it for at least 30 days? Do you understand its features and limitations?
*   **Audience Match:** Is the pricing, complexity, and feature set appropriate for your audience of operators? A tool built for agencies might be overkill for a solo consultant.
*   **Vendor Reputation:** Does the company offer reliable support? Are there frequent outages? Check independent reviews, not just testimonials on their site.
*   **Commission Structure:** Is the commission recurring (e.g., monthly subscription percentage) or a one-time payment? Recurring commissions build more predictable income.
*   **Cookie Duration:** How long does the cookie last? A 60-day or 90-day cookie gives your audience more time to decide after clicking your link. Less than 30 days can be difficult to convert.

Here's a comparison of two hypothetical tool types to illustrate evaluation points:

| Criteria             | Basic Rank Tracker                               | Advanced SEO Suite                                   |
| :------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------- |
| **Price Point**      | $10-50/month (entry-level)                       | $100-500+/month (mid-to-enterprise)                  |
| **Primary Use Case** | Daily keyword position checks, competitor tracking | All-in-one keyword, content, link, technical analysis |
| **Target User**      | Solo operators, small businesses, specific project | Agencies, larger in-house teams, multiple projects   |
| **Commission Type**  | Often recurring percentage                       | Mix of recurring and one-time, higher payout         |
| **Support Quality**  | Usually ticket-based, slower responses           | Dedicated account managers, faster resolution        |

Choose the tool that best aligns with your audience's current needs and budget. For AuthorityForge, an operator-focused site, tools that offer direct, measurable impact without excessive complexity often perform well.

## Disclosure placement

Transparency is non-negotiable when recommending affiliate products. Proper disclosure builds trust and complies with regulations like the FTC's Endorsement Guides in the U.S. Your disclosure must be "clear and conspicuous."

Here's how to implement effective disclosure:

1.  **Above the Fold:** For dedicated review pages or articles heavily featuring affiliate links, place a disclosure statement at the very top of the content, before any scrolling is required.
2.  **Within the First Paragraph:** For shorter blog posts or sections that mention an affiliate product early, integrate a disclosure within the first one or two paragraphs.
3.  **Near the Link:** If you mention a tool and link to it mid-paragraph, a short parenthetical or asterisked note immediately following the link can work. Example: "I use Tool X for this task (affiliate link*)."
4.  **Dedicated Disclosure Page:** Maintain a site-wide affiliate disclosure page. Link to this page from your footer and consider linking to it from within your main content disclosures. AuthorityForge's own `/legal/affiliate-disclosure/` is a good example.

**Example Disclosure Wording:**

*   **Short:** "This post contains affiliate links. If you make a purchase, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you."
*   **Detailed:** "Please note: Some links in this article are affiliate links. This means if you click and make a purchase, I might receive a small commission. This doesn't affect the price you pay, and I only recommend products I genuinely use and trust."

The goal is that no reader should ever be surprised to learn you're an affiliate. Make it obvious, but don't disrupt the reading experience with overly long disclaimers.

## Tool categories that convert

Successful affiliate promotions for SEO operators often align with common, recurring tasks in their daily workflows. These aren't just "nice-to-have" tools; they are productivity enhancers or essential components of a project.
