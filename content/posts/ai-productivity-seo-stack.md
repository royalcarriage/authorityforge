**Direct answer:** Let AI draft and classify; keep humans or frontier models for final publish, schema accuracy, and deploy. Never auto-index raw generations.

## Suggested split

- **Local / cheap models:** audit lists, rewrites, clustering drafts
- **Cloud frontier:** architecture, final copy, PR/deploy

## Guardrails

One owner URL per intent, disclosure on affiliates, no fabricated stats.
