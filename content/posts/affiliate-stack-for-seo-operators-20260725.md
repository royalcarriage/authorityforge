**Direct answer:** When creating affiliate content as an SEO operator, it's crucial to select tools that genuinely fit into your workflow and benefit your audience. This approach not only enhances the credibility of your recommendations but also helps in building trust with your readers.

## Criteria before links

Before linking to any affiliate products or services, ensure you have a clear understanding of their relevance to your niche. Evaluate whether these tools are genuinely useful based on your experience and the needs of your audience. Avoid pushing affiliates that don't align with your expertise or content focus purely for monetary gain.

## Disclosure placement

Transparency is key when it comes to affiliate links. Always disclose your affiliation in a clear and visible manner, preferably near the beginning of your article where readers can easily see it. This practice not only adheres to ethical guidelines but also builds trust by being upfront about the financial incentives involved.

## Tool categories that convert

Focus on promoting tools within specific categories that naturally align with SEO practices such as keyword research, content optimization, backlink analysis, and social media management. By sticking to these areas, you can provide value through relevant recommendations while maintaining credibility with your audience.

## What not to promote

Avoid promoting generic or irrelevant products that do not add genuine value to the reader's experience. This includes anything outside of SEO practices unless it has a direct impact on improving search engine rankings or content creation quality. Be selective and thoughtful about what you endorse as an affiliate marketer.

## Weekly revenue review

Regularly reviewing your weekly earnings can help you understand which affiliates are performing well and which ones might need adjustment. Use this data to refine your strategy, focusing more on the tools that generate higher returns while maintaining relevance and usefulness for your audience.
