# Your First 7 Days in the Forge Dashboard

Getting started with a new SEO platform can feel like a climb. This guide outlines your first week in AuthorityForge, focusing on immediate, practical steps. We'll move you from signup to your first AI brief and an understanding of how to generate revenue, without getting lost in theory.

## Day 0 Signup

Your journey begins with account creation. This step is straightforward and quick.

Navigate to the AuthorityForge homepage. Click the "Sign Up Free" button, typically located in the top right corner. You'll be prompted to enter your email address and create a password. Complete the CAPTCHA if presented.

After submitting your details, check your inbox for a verification email. Click the link within this email to confirm your account. This action activates your dashboard access. You are now ready to log in and begin setting up your first project.

## Create Project

A "project" in AuthorityForge represents a specific website or domain you manage. Setting one up is your next immediate task.

Once logged in, look for a prominent "New Project" button. It's usually central on the dashboard or in the left-hand navigation. Click it.

You'll see a form asking for project details.
1.  **Project Name:** Enter a descriptive name. For instance, "My Main Blog" or "Client Site A."
2.  **Domain:** Input the primary domain URL for your website (e.g., `example.com`). Do not include `https://` or `www` unless specifically requested by the input field. The system typically handles these variations.

Click "Create Project." The dashboard will then load your new project's overview. This structure keeps your data organized, allowing you to manage multiple sites efficiently.

## Three Checklist Items

Every new project benefits from foundational setup tasks. AuthorityForge provides a checklist to guide you. Focus on these three critical items first.

You'll find the project checklist on your project's main dashboard screen. Locate and complete these specific actions:

1.  **Connect Google Search Console (GSC):**
    *   **Action:** Click the "Connect GSC" button.
    *   **Procedure:** You'll be redirected to Google to authorize AuthorityForge access to your GSC data. Select the correct Google account and property. This connection brings in real search performance data, which is vital for keyword tracking and content gap analysis.
2.  **Define Target Audience Persona:**
    *   **Action:** Click "Define Persona."
    *   **Procedure:** A small form will appear. Input details about your ideal reader or customer. For example: "Outdoor enthusiasts, aged 25-45, interested in hiking and camping gear, value durability and performance." This helps the AI understand your content's direction.
3.  **Add Initial Seed Keywords:**
    *   **Action:** Click "Add Keywords."
    *   **Procedure:** Enter 3-5 broad keywords related to your site's main topics. Examples for an outdoor blog might be: "hiking boots," "camping tents," "backpacking gear." These seed keywords kickstart the platform's ability to suggest relevant content ideas and track your competitive landscape.

Completing these three items provides the basic data AuthorityForge needs to start delivering relevant insights and content suggestions.

## First AI Brief

Generating an AI content brief is where AuthorityForge's productivity power becomes clear. This tool helps outline content quickly.

Navigate to the "AI Briefs" section in your project's sidebar menu. Click the "New Brief" button.

You'll be prompted to enter a target keyword or topic.
*   **Example Keyword:** "best lightweight hiking boots for beginners"

Input your chosen keyword and click "Generate Brief." The system will then process this request.

Within moments, AuthorityForge's AI will produce a detailed content brief. This brief typically includes:
*   **Target Word Count:** An estimated length for the article.
*   **Key Headings (H2s, H3s):** A suggested structure for your content.
*   **Important Entities/Keywords:** Terms and phrases to include for topical authority.
*   **Competitor Analysis:** Insights into what top-ranking pages cover.
*   **Audience Questions:** Common questions users ask about the topic.

This brief acts as a blueprint for your content writer, ensuring the article covers important points and is optimized for search. Review the brief, make any necessary edits directly in the interface, and then you can export it or share it with your content team.

## Affiliate Path Preview

AuthorityForge is designed to help SEOs generate revenue, and affiliate marketing is a direct path for many. The platform offers tools to support this.

In your dashboard, look for the "Affiliate Tools" section in the left-hand navigation. Click on it.

You'll typically find features here to assist with affiliate content creation and link management. While specific tools may vary, a common example is a "Link Builder."

*   **Example Tool: Link Builder**
    *   **Purpose:** Quickly generate affiliate links for various platforms (e.g., Amazon, shareasale).
    *   **Procedure:** Select your desired affiliate program. Input a product ASIN or URL. The tool generates a properly formatted affiliate link, often with tracking parameters.
    *   **Benefit:** Streamlines the process of monetizing product reviews or recommendations within your content.

Another common feature might be an "Affiliate Product Finder." This tool helps you discover relevant products on affiliate networks based on your niche and keywords. Exploring these tools early helps you plan your content strategy with monetization in mind.
